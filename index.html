USING YOUR BENEFITS

How it works

01
Register as a member

Create your Circle account in a minute. Parents and guardians register with their child's personal number, and BIST staff with their work email.

02
Choose an offer

Browse or search the offers below. Each one shows what you get and how long the offer runs.

03
Visit the partner

Go directly to the partner, or book with them where the offer says so.

04
Activate your benefit

Every offer says how to identify yourself: usually your BIST parent card, sometimes a promo code or a mention of Circle when booking. See "How to claim" in the offer details.

Exclusively for the BIST community. Circle is the parent community of the British International School of Tbilisi, so every offer is reserved for registered BIST families. To use one, follow the "How to claim" steps in the offer: your BIST parent card, a promo code or a mention of Circle when you book.
